Sources and provenance. The vowel set follows the English Wikipedia "Niqqud" article as the canonical reference; every mark, name, and sound was checked against its chart (see the verification pass). Two deliberate departures from the source are recorded here:

 - Notation. Wikipedia gives each vowel's pronunciation as an IPA transcription in square brackets — IPA's convention for a phonetic transcription. The cards drop the brackets: the bracketed form competes visually with the pictogram and crowds the reveal. Only the notation is simplified; the values are the same IPA, and the transcription still describes the vowel's actual sound.

 - A non-canonical entry. One form — card 020, Hiriq — is not in the Wikipedia chart. It was taught in class as a rare usage and kept for completeness.
